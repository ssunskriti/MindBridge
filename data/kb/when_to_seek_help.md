---
title: When to Seek Professional Help and Helplines in India
source: MoHFW Tele-MANAS (National Tele Mental Health Programme); Government of India emergency services
url: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in
retrieved: 2026-09
---
It is a good idea to talk to a counsellor or doctor if low mood, anxiety, sleep problems or stress last for two weeks or more, if they affect your studies, eating, sleep or relationships, or if you find it hard to cope day to day. Asking for help is a sign of strength, not weakness. Tele-MANAS is the free, 24x7 national tele mental health helpline of the Government of India, reachable at 14416 or 1-800-891-4416, with support in many Indian languages. In an emergency or if someone is in immediate danger, call 112. Most universities also have a counselling cell where students can book confidential appointments. MindBridge can help you raise a counsellor request, including anonymously.
