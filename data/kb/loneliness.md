Feeling lonely or homesick is very common, especially in the first year or after moving to a new city. It does not mean something is wrong with you. Small, regular contact helps more than waiting for one big friendship: say hello to people on your floor, sit with classmates in the mess, or join one club, sports team or study group. Shared activities make conversation easier. Stay in touch with family and old friends with a short call or message. Spending some time in common areas instead of always in your room increases chances to connect. Many universities run peer mentoring or buddy programmes. If loneliness feels heavy for a long time or comes with low mood, talking to a counsellor can help.
