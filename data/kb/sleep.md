---
title: Healthy Sleep Habits for Students
source: Summarised by MindBridge team from public sleep-hygiene guidance (WHO / national health services)
url: https://www.who.int
retrieved: 2026-09
---
Most young adults need about 7 to 9 hours of sleep. Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day, including weekends, helps set the body clock. Bright screens and scrolling late at night delay sleep, so put the phone away 30 to 45 minutes before bed and keep it out of reach. Avoid caffeine such as tea, coffee and energy drinks in the late afternoon and evening. Keep the room dark, quiet and cool; earplugs or an eye mask can help in shared hostel rooms. If you cannot fall asleep after about 20 minutes, get up, do something calm in dim light and go back when sleepy. Slow breathing exercises can relax the body in bed. Long daytime naps can make night sleep harder. If sleep problems continue for several weeks and affect daily life, speak to a doctor or counsellor.
