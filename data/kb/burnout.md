Feeling burnt out, drained or exhausted for weeks can be a sign of burnout. Burnout is described by the WHO as resulting from chronic stress that has not been successfully managed, with feelings of exhaustion, mental distance or cynicism, and reduced effectiveness. Students can feel similar exhaustion from continuous classes, assignments, clubs and internships. Recovery starts with reducing load: list your commitments and postpone or drop at least one. Schedule real rest with no study. Regular sleep, meals and physical activity rebuild energy. Set realistic daily goals instead of trying to do everything. Talk to faculty or mentors about deadlines if needed. If exhaustion continues for weeks or comes with persistent low mood, please speak to a counsellor.
