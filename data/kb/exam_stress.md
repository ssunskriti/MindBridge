Some stress before exams is normal and can even help focus, but too much stress makes it hard to concentrate and remember. Break study material into small, specific tasks and plan them across the days you have, instead of trying to cover everything at once. Study in short focused blocks, such as 25 minutes of work followed by a 5 minute break. Practise with past papers under timed conditions so the real exam feels familiar. Sleep is part of studying: memory is strengthened during sleep, so all-night study sessions often reduce performance. Keep eating regular meals and drink water. When worry thoughts appear, notice them, name them as worry, and gently bring attention back to the task. Talking to a teacher, mentor or batchmate about a difficult topic is often faster than struggling alone. If exam stress is stopping you from functioning for weeks, speaking with the university counsellor is a good step.
